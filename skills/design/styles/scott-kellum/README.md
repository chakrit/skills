# Scott Kellum research

Research behind `style.md`: what Scott Kellum (Typetura; formerly Vox Media and Darden
Studio) has published openly about how he designs, what his own and client sites show,
and what others report about his method, analysed for design thinking, type and taste
rather than code. Gathered November 2026 from freely available sources only: his site and
case studies, his public class syllabus, free articles, public talk transcripts and
slides, written interviews, live client sites, and Fonts In Use entries.

| Path           | Contents                                                       |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `style.md`     | The style's instructions                                       |
| `previews/`    | Self-contained pages showing the style                         |
| `synthesis.md` | The design model drawn from all four streams, citing each note |
| `notes/`       | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                  |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `own.md`      | His site today and four earlier versions, its CSS, essays, his class    |
| `work.md`     | Client and product work 2008–2025: Vox, Typetura, Ishmael, A New Social |
| `talks.md`    | "Mapping Typography" and other talks, podcasts and written interviews   |
| `accounts.md` | Write-ups by other people, reader critiques, Fonts In Use, tool reviews |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him, observed in the work, read from CSS
or markup only, or reported by someone else. His current site, typetura.com, several
client sites and his Roslindale specimen page were looked at in a browser on 2026-11-23.
`talks.md` and `accounts.md` were gathered without a browser. The Wayback Machine blocked
access part way through, so only four old home pages were seen and his 2013–2015 posts
were read as excerpts; Vox.com and SB Nation as he designed them were not seen. The notes
cite raw page text under `/tmp/designers/scott-kellum/`, which was a local working
directory and is not included; each note lists its source URLs, so any page can be
fetched again.
