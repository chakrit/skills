Present a show

Presenting takes two free hours a week and four Tuesday evenings in the chapel, where Mags Tierney teaches the desk, the law (mostly about swearing before nine) and how to talk to one person instead of a crowd.

After that, pick a blank hour. Overnight is the easiest slot to get and the hardest to keep: three people have tried 3am since July, and one of them now does breakfast. Wednesday afternoon has been empty since Colin’s hip.

The next open evening is Tuesday 1 December at 7pm, 212 North Street. Or write to volunteer@bedminsterwireless.org.uk with the hour you want and what you’d do with it.
