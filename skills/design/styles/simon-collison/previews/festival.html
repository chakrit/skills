A day of very quiet music in a draughty hall Lowdale Mechanics’ Institute · Saturday 14 March Slow Air

Five sets of ambient and field-recorded music and two talks, from eleven in the morning until the last bus. The hall holds 180 people on chairs and about forty more lying on the floor for the evening sets.

Getting to Lowdale

By train and bus

Trains run to Hebden Bridge from Leeds and Manchester about twice an hour. The 593 leaves from the station forecourt at twenty past each hour and takes twenty-five minutes up the valley. Ask for the Institute; every driver knows it.

By car

There are twelve spaces behind the hall and more at the Fleece, who have kindly agreed to let us use their car park in exchange for the lunch trade. The lane is single track for the last mile. Reverse politely.

What to bring

A jumper. The Institute is heated by those radiators and by the audience, and neither is quick. A mat or a cushion if you plan to lie down for the evening sets. Cash for the urn; there is an honesty tin.

Getting in

£28

For the whole day, talks included. Under-18s pay £12 and children under ten come free with an adult. Tickets are sold by the Lowdale Village Hall Trust and any money left over goes on the roof, which needs it.

If you can no longer come, write to us by 7 March at tickets@slowair.example.org and we will refund you or pass your seat on.
