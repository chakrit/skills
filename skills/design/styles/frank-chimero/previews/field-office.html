We mostly work on buildings that are already standing.

Field Office is three architects in a hut on Mill Lane. The county built it in 1960 so its surveyors had somewhere dry to unroll drawings. We bought it in 2014, mended the roof and kept the rest.

Most of what we do is like that. A chapel becomes a library, a dairy becomes four homes, a school gets a hall it can heat. We take on about eight jobs a year, and every one of them is drawn and run by one of the three of us from the first visit to the handover.

Starting a job
What happens after you write

  1. Tell us about the building and where it is. A few phone photos are plenty.
  2. One of us comes to look at it, free, within two weeks if it is inside the county.
  3. We send you a letter with what we would do first, roughly what it costs, and our fee.
