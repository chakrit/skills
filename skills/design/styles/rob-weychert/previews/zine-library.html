Two Staples

A free reading room and lending library of 6,140 zines made in and around Toronto, one flight up from a laundromat on Bloor Street West. Come and read, borrow five at a time, make one with us, or fill a gap on our shelves.

Ring the buzzer marked TS beside the laundromat door. Dufferin station is a seven-minute walk east of us; the 40 bus stops at the corner.

There are nineteen stairs and no lift. Call 416-555-0142 from the sidewalk and someone will bring a box of whatever you ask for down to the bench outside.

Reading is free and needs no card. The kettle is also free.

Missing issues

Eight Toronto series that ran from 1979 to 2016 printed 252 issues between them. We hold 169. Each mark below is one issue, in order: orange is on our shelves, an outline is one we have never seen.

If a hole lines up with something in your closet, email holes@twostaples.ca with the title and issue number. We scan loans and post them back within the month.

Borrowing

Anyone with a Toronto address can borrow five zines for three weeks. Bring something with the address on it; a hydro bill works, and so does a letter from your mum.

Some zines stay in the room: first issues, anything with a cassette taped inside, and anything the maker asked us not to lend. Those are marked with a red dot on the spine, and you are welcome to sit with them as long as you like.

There are no late fees. If you lose one, tell us which, and we will add it to the list above.
