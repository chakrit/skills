# Frank Chimero research

Research behind `style.md`: what Frank Chimero has published openly about how he designs,
what his own and client work shows, and what others report about it, analysed for design
thinking and taste rather than code. Gathered November 2026 from freely available sources
only: his sites and essays, talks as published on his site, interviews and podcast
transcripts, live client and studio pages, and the free online edition of *The Shape of
Design* (CC BY-NC-SA, paraphrased, never reproduced).

| Path           | Contents                                                       |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `style.md`     | The style's instructions                                       |
| `previews/`    | Self-contained pages showing the style                         |
| `synthesis.md` | The design model drawn from all four streams, citing each note |
| `notes/`       | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                     |
|---------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `own.md`      | His sites 2011 to now, art-directed essays, *The Shape of Design*, essays  |
| `work.md`     | Client, studio and product work 2008–2025: Atlantis, TFAL, Studio Frank …  |
| `talks.md`    | Talks as published on his site, their slides, interviews and transcripts   |
| `accounts.md` | Peers, reviewers and readers on his work and ideas, including dissent      |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him, observed in the work, or reported by
someone else. Live pages were looked at in a browser on 2026-11-23. Some earlier site
versions, the art-directed essays and dead client sites were seen in Internet Archive
captures before the research was told to stop using Wayback; those findings are marked
"(Wayback)" and no more were fetched. No YouTube or talk video was used. The notes cite
raw page text under `/tmp/designers/frank-chimero/`, which was a local working directory
and is not included; each note lists its source URLs, so any page can be fetched again.
