Size and timber

1200 mm wide, 400 mm high, 300 mm deep
Each bay
376 mm wide inside, between 18 mm oak boards
Timber
English oak from Gaukroger’s yard in Mytholmroyd
Finish
Hard wax oil, one coat inside, two outside
Weight
19 kg open to 26 kg with three drawer bays
Hanging
On an oak French cleat, which comes with it
Making
Seven weeks from your order

Ordering

Email the code and your address to orders@wrenlane.example. We reply with a date and ask for half the price; the other half is due when the cabinet is on your wall.

We deliver and hang it ourselves within sixty miles of Hebden Bridge. Further than that, it goes by carrier with the cleat and a paper template for the screw holes.
