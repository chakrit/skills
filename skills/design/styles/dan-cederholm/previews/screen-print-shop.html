Off Register is two presses, a drying rack, a darkroom the size of a coat closet and three people who mostly agree about ink. We have printed in the River Arts District since 2009, in what used to be a tire shop. You can still smell the tires on a hot day.

The name comes from our first paying job. The red slid two millimeters to the left on all 140 shirts, the band liked it, and we have been explaining it at parties ever since. We have since bought a better hinge clamp.
