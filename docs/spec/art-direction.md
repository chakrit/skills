# art-direction

Status: implemented

The `art-direction` skill's design, for agents editing the skill.

## Sources and scope

- The skill is published. It uses no material from Andy Clarke's paid books. Book
  content supplied later goes to an unpublished place, never into this skill.
- Book ideas may enter when other people mention or blog about them, or when he covers
  them in his own blogs or interviews.
- Without his books, learn from accounts of people who worked with him, his reference
  sites, and the sites clients hired him for.
- Analyse his design layout, how he structures his thinking, and his taste; his code
  patterns matter little.
- Write in our own words with short attributed quotations, and describe his moves as
  principles rather than reproducing his designs.
- `SKILL.md` and `references/moves.md` hold generic instructions usable on any site. The
  research under `references/` is kept as written for AppBaoBao, a studio building sites
  for Thai small businesses.
