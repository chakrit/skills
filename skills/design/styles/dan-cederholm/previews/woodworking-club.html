Woodwork under the arches

The Holbeck Shavings Club has met on Thursday nights since 1977, in a railway arch off Water Lane with eleven benches and more clamps than anyone has counted. About forty of us are members. Some build chairs; one has been building the same bookcase since 2019. Beginners are welcome, and if you have a rusty plane from a car boot sale, bring it in and we'll see if it can be saved.

  • Thursdays 6.30 to 9.30pm, and Saturday mornings in term
  • Where Arch 14, Water Lane, LS11. Ten minutes from Leeds station. Knock loudly; the extractor is on.
  • Cost First visit free, then £8 a month or £3 a night

On the wall in the arch

Everything here is shared. Most of it was donated. The sash cramps turned up in 1984 and nobody knows who brought them.
