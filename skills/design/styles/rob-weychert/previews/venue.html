The Undercroft

Two rooms under the Kessler laundrette. The main room holds 160 and the back bar sixty. Main-room doors open at half seven and back-bar doors at eight, and everyone is out by eleven because the flats upstairs have small children.

14 Rennick Street, Leeds LS6. The door is the green one to the left of the laundrette.

Getting in

Advance tickets are sold here and behind the bar. On the door they cost two pounds more, cash or card.

The main room is down twenty-two steps from Rennick Street and there is no lift. The street runs downhill, so the back bar opens level onto the yard behind the laundrette. If stairs are a problem, email door@undercroft.example before the night and we will meet you at the yard gate; from there the main room is eight steps down.

The Undercroft

Each show takes its colour from its date. The hue moves thirty degrees a month and one degree a day, so winter shows come out blue and violet and summer shows run from yellow through orange to red. Shows we have played fade to grey.

Set in Anybody by Etcetera Type Co., one family throughout, wide for the small type and narrow for the names.

The Undercroft, Rennick Street and every band here are fictional. This page is a Rob Weychert style preview.
