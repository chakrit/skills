We are two walkers with a small press on Stramongate in Kendal. Since 2009 we have been drawing the hills east of the town, from Kentmere out to the Howgills, and printing them as pocket guides that fit a jacket and outlast the rain. Every route in them has been walked at least twice, in different seasons, by one of us with a notebook.

Ruth Atkinson and Tom Lowis, Kentside Guides. The shop is a ten-minute walk from Kendal station, over the river.

Four guides in print, each one an area you can reach from Kendal by bus or a short drive

  1. The Kentmere Round

    18 routes · 112 pages · £12.50

    The full horseshoe over Ill Bell and Thornthwaite Crag, and the quieter days up Longsleddale for when you only have an afternoon. The winter loop along Haweswater is the one we walk most.

  2. The Howgills

    22 routes · 128 pages · £13.00

    Round, grassy and nearly without walls. Walks from Sedbergh and Cautley, the long ridge to The Calf, and two days end to end with a night in Ravenstonedale.

  3. Whitbarrow and the Lyth Valley

    15 routes · 96 pages · £11.00

    Limestone pavement and old yews above the damson orchards of the Lyth Valley. Short days for when the high fells are in cloud; most are under eight miles.

  4. Kendal on Foot

    20 walks · 80 pages · £9.00

    Every walk starts at the station or a bus stop in town. Scout Scar at sunset is the favourite, and the canal towpath to Crooklands is flat enough for a pushchair.

How a guide is made

One of us walks a route with a notebook and a barometer and writes it down stile by stile. A few months later, in a different season, the other walks it again from the notes alone, and wherever they hesitate the text gets rewritten. Paths move; a gate that was open in July is chained in February, and that has to be in the book.

Tom draws the maps and ridge profiles by hand from the walk and prints them in the old map inks, with the path in magenta. Pages are sewn rather than glued, so a guide opens flat on a wall in the wind.

The shop on Stramongate

The press and the shop are the same room, and we keep the one-inch maps for every area we cover. If it is raining, ask us: we will tell you which walk in the book stays below the cloud.

Ordering by post

Write to orders@kentsideguides.co.uk with the guides you want and an address. We post on Tuesdays and Fridays, and postage is £2.40 however many you order. When a route changes we put a correction slip in every copy we send out, and anyone who bought the guide can ask for one.
