Drafts

We keep every draft. This is the first line of The Walnut Tree, from Ilka Rónai’s Hungarian to the sentence we printed in 2009.

  1. 1 Ilka Rónai, 2004 A kert végében állt egy diófa, és senki sem emlékezett, ki ültette.
  2. 2 Zsófia, first pass At the end of the garden there stood a walnut tree, and nobody remembered who had planted it.
  3. 3 Owen, read aloud A walnut tree stood at the bottom of the garden, and no one could remember who had planted it.
  4. 4 Printed, 2009 At the bottom of the garden stood a walnut tree that nobody remembered planting.
