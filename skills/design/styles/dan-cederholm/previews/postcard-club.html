How a round works

One card a month, to someone you have never met. You find out who wrote to you when it lands.

  1. 1

    Sign up for the round

    Rounds open on the first of each month. Skip any month you like.

    By the 1st
  2. 2

    Get an address

    We email you one address, chosen at random from this round’s members. Someone else gets yours.

    The 2nd
  3. 3

    Mail your outbound card

    Draw it, print it, stitch it or buy it at a gas station. Second-class postage is fine.

    By the 20th
  4. 4

    Post your inbound card

    When a card arrives, photograph the front and put it on the sorting table with your stranger’s town.

    Whenever
