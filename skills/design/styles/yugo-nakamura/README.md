# Yugo Nakamura research

Research behind `style.md`: what Yugo Nakamura (中村勇吾, tha ltd.) has said in public about
how he designs, what tha's own sites and project pages show, and what juries, critics and
collaborators report, analysed for design thinking, motion, type and taste rather than
code. Gathered November 2026 from freely available sources only: tha.jp and yugop.com,
tha's project texts and credits, published stills, free interviews, dialogues, talk
reports and podcast transcripts (mostly Japanese, quoted briefly in our own translation),
and free reviews and award texts.

| Path           | Contents                                                       |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `style.md`     | The style's instructions                                       |
| `previews/`    | Self-contained pages showing the style                         |
| `synthesis.md` | The design model drawn from all four streams, citing each note |
| `notes/`       | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                 |
|---------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `own.md`      | tha.jp today, its theme CSS and script, yugop.com's earlier versions   |
| `work.md`     | Client and own-label work 2003–2025 from tha.jp pages and credits      |
| `talks.md`    | Interviews, dialogues, talk reports and podcast transcripts, 2001–2026 |
| `accounts.md` | Juries, critics, reviewers and collaborators on the work and method    |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him (or by tha), observed in the work, or
reported by someone else. The `own` and `work` streams looked at live pages and published
stills in a browser; `talks` and `accounts` did not use a browser. Most of tha's Flash-era
work no longer runs and is documented on tha.jp only by YouTube videos, which were out of
scope, so those pieces rest on tha's descriptions and credits. A few pages (yugop.com's
earlier versions, tha.jp in 2009 and 2014, the UNIQLOCK hub, two interviews) were read
through the Wayback Machine before chakrit ruled archive workarounds out on 2026-11-23;
nothing further was taken from the archive. `work.md` separates pieces Nakamura directed
from tha pieces credited to colleagues. The notes cite raw page text under
`/tmp/designers/yugo-nakamura/raw/`, which was a local working directory and is not
included; each note lists its source URLs, so any page can be fetched again.
