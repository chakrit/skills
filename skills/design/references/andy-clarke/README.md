# Andy Clarke research

Research behind `docs/spec/art-direction.md`: what Andy Clarke (Stuff & Nonsense) has
published openly about how he designs, and what others report about working with him,
analysed for design thinking, layout and taste rather than code. Gathered November 2026
from freely available sources only; no paid books or members-only material.

| Path            | Contents                                                                |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `synthesis.md`  | The design model drawn from all five streams, citing each note          |
| `notes/`        | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list             |
| `digests/`      | Per-issue and per-demo detail behind `notes/idd.md`; podcast digest     |
| `BRIEF.md`      | The brief the research agents worked from                               |
| `scripts/`      | One-off fetch and screenshot helpers, kept as a record of the method    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                    |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `idd.md`      | The "Inspired Design Decisions" Smashing articles and 40 weekly demos     |
| `own.md`      | His blog 2004–2026, his own site's redesigns, free resources and sample   |
| `clients.md`  | 26+ client sites: where each concept came from, layouts, phone versions   |
| `talks.md`    | Talks, guest articles, interviews and podcast transcripts                 |
| `accounts.md` | Testimonials, reviews, critiques and comment threads by other people      |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him, observed in the work, or reported by
someone else. The notes cite screenshots and raw page text under `tmp/andy-clarke/`;
those copies are local working files and are not committed. Each note lists the source
URLs, so any page can be fetched again. The scripts hold absolute paths from the machine
they ran on and are not maintained tools.
