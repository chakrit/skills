# Jason Santa Maria research

Research behind `style.md`: what Jason Santa Maria has published openly about how he
designs, what his own and client work shows, and what others report about working with
him, analysed for design thinking, type and taste rather than code. Gathered November
2026 from freely available sources only: his sites, free articles, public talk records,
live and Wayback-archived work, and *On Web Typography*, which he offers as a free
download under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (paraphrased, never reproduced).

| Path           | Contents                                                       |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `style.md`     | The style's instructions                                       |
| `previews/`    | Self-contained pages showing the style                         |
| `synthesis.md` | The design model drawn from all four streams, citing each note |
| `notes/`       | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                   |
|---------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `own.md`      | His sites v1 to v5 and today, 63 v4 and 47 v5 posts, essays, the book    |
| `work.md`     | Client and product work 2005–2026: ALA, Happy Cog, Slate, Loud Room …    |
| `talks.md`    | Talks (via liveblogs), his articles, interviews and podcast transcripts  |
| `accounts.md` | Peers, reviewers and collaborators on his work and method                |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him, observed in the work, or reported by
someone else. The streams were first gathered without a browser, from CSS, markup, image
files and write-ups. On 2026-11-23 about 20 v4 and v5 posts, his current and v5 home
pages, and Wayback or live captures of A List Apart, Happy Cog, AIGA, Comhaltas, Slate,
Typekit, Typedia, Editorially, The Morning News, Bobulate, A Book Apart, Loud Room and The
Type Founders were checked in a browser; claims confirmed there carry "seen 2026-11-23",
and corrections say so. `accounts.md` remains second-hand. The notes cite raw page text
under `/tmp/jsm/`, which was a local working directory and is not included; each note
lists its source URLs, so any page can be fetched again.
