Hot sauce from a rented kitchen behind a laundromat in Tucson.

Volt Pepper Co. is Rosa Ybarra and Ken Duvall. We make about two hundred bottles a batch and ferment the peppers for six weeks in crocks that used to hold Rosa’s grandmother’s pickles. Every label goes on by hand at the folding table next to the dryers.

Markets and mail

  • Barrio Sur growers’ market on Saturdays, 8 to noon
  • The Grant Road feed store lot on Sundays, 9 to 1
  • Any three bottles by mail for $30, shipped Mondays
  • When we sell out, the next batch is usually three weeks away

Batch notes

When a batch comes out of the crocks we send one email saying what is in it and how hot it came out. Batch 44 had a lot of rain on the chiltepin and runs a little milder.

The laundromat owner takes the first bottle of every batch as part of our rent.
