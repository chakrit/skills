# Rob Weychert research

Research behind `style.md`: what Rob Weychert has published openly about how he designs,
what his own and client work shows, and what others report about working with him,
analysed for design thinking, type and taste rather than code. Gathered November 2026
from freely available sources only: his seven site versions (all still live on `v1` to
`v7` subdomains of robweychert.com), his blog and project write-ups, a free talk text,
public podcast transcripts, live client work and public pages by others. No paid books
or members-only material.

| Path           | Contents                                                       |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `style.md`     | The style's instructions                                       |
| `previews/`    | Self-contained pages showing the style                         |
| `synthesis.md` | The design model drawn from all four streams, citing each note |
| `notes/`       | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                      |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `own.md`      | His sites V2 to V7 and about 35 design posts, 2005–2026                     |
| `work.md`     | ProPublica, The Markup, Comhaltas, Plus Equals, Robtober, title sequences … |
| `talks.md`    | Talks 2007–2018: SND talk text, slides, attendee notes; one podcast         |
| `accounts.md` | His design director, podcast hosts, award notices, Fonts In Use credits     |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him, observed in the work, or reported by
someone else. On 2026-11-23 his V7 home and project pages, V6, V4, V3 and V2, Plus Equals,
Robtober 2021, Lost Mothers and two Beyond Tellerrand project pages were checked in a
browser; V5 was read from HTML and CSS only. `work.md` also used two Wayback captures
(The Markup homepage, Comhaltas) opened before the no-Wayback rule took effect, and
`talks.md` read the 2007 Design Interviews Q&A through Wayback; no other archive
workarounds were used. `talks.md` and `accounts.md` used no browser and are text-only.
The notes cite raw page text under `/tmp/designers/rob-weychert/raw/`, which was a local
working directory and is not included; each note lists its source URLs, so any page can
be fetched again.
