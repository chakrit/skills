Four generations

Walter Pruitt opened the store in 1927 with a keg of nails and a scale from the feed mill. His great-granddaughter June runs it now. The scale is still on the counter, and it is still how we sell nails.

If you need one hinge, June will sell you one hinge and show you which way round it goes.
