Shoal Creek Sound makes podcasts in a converted tire shop on the east side of Austin. Three of us record, edit and mix about forty series a year, most of them for people with a story worth an hour of your attention and no settled idea of how an episode should run. We help decide that before anyone sits at a microphone, then spend most of our hours in the edit, cutting until the tape keeps moving.

Room A is the old tire bay, lined and floated

The booth is fourteen feet by eighteen, built as a room inside the room on rubber pucks, with a window onto the control desk. It seats four around a walnut table. Guests talk into Shure SM7Bs on short arms, and the desk records to two machines at once, so a crashed laptop has never cost anyone a take.

You can book it with an engineer by the hour or the day. Parking is in the gravel lot behind the building; the 2 bus stops at the corner. There is a kettle, real milk, and a dog called Ampex who stays out of the booth.
