Two Drum, risograph studio in Port Ellery

Two Drum is a risograph studio on Wharf Lane in Port Ellery. We print zines, gig posters, menus and seed packets in runs of fifty to two thousand, on a machine that lays each ink down through its own stencil, two drums to a pass, so inks printed over each other make a third colour.

Ash and Ines run the studio and answer every email themselves.

Recent jobs

  1. Low Tide, issue 9

    The harbour club’s swimmers make this zine twice a year. The sea is one pink halftone screen; we printed the yellow sun a millimetre off its outline because they asked for it to look like a reflection.

    A5 · 28 pages · pink, yellow · 300 copies

  2. Cinder Rooms, spring season

    For the venue on Mill Street we printed a spring season of posters, one band per sheet, with letters cut from card and laid straight on the scanner.

    A2 · 6 designs · teal, pink · 150 of each

  3. Marrow & Salt menus

    A fish restaurant on the quay changes its menu every Friday, so we reprint the cards that morning: pink for the dishes, yellow for whatever came off the boats.

    A4 · pink, yellow · 80 a week

  4. Hedgerow Seed Library packets

    Volunteers at the community seed bank drew all twelve plants in pencil. We split each drawing into two inks, and the leaves came out green where the teal sits on the yellow.

    C6 · 12 designs · teal, yellow · 2,000 packets

Five drums

The machine holds two drums at once. Changing one takes about fifteen minutes, so pink and yellow stay in for the Friday menus and we group jobs in the other inks into their own weeks. A third ink means a second pass, and the paper can shift by up to a millimetre between passes; we leave room for that in the artwork.

Come by the studio

Bring the drawing on paper if you have one. We scan it on the bed, split it into inks with you, and pull a proof before the run.

To send artwork, make a PDF at print size with one greyscale page per ink, drawings at 600 dpi. If all you have is a colour file, send that and we split it into inks for £10.
