# Simon Collison research

Research behind `style.md`: what Simon Collison has published openly about how he
designs, what his own sites and client work show, and what others report about his work,
analysed for design thinking, type and taste rather than code. Gathered November 2026 from
freely available sources only: his site and journal at colly.com (including the
self-hosted `/v4/` archive), his case studies, free articles on A List Apart and 24 ways,
public slide decks on Speaker Deck, Notist and SlideShare, event pages, interviews, and
other people's blog posts and comments.

| Path           | Contents                                                       |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `style.md`     | The style's instructions                                       |
| `previews/`    | Self-contained pages showing the style                         |
| `synthesis.md` | The design model drawn from all four streams, citing each note |
| `notes/`       | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                        |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `own.md`      | His sites 2004 to now: CollyLogic, the v4 miscellany, the 2019 timeline       |
| `work.md`     | Erskine Design, Frieze, New Adventures, Rushmore, TeuxDeux, Samsung, Citipo … |
| `talks.md`    | Talk decks 2010–2018 by slide text, his articles, interviews                  |
| `accounts.md` | Comments on his 2010 redesign, conference reviews, client and peer quotes     |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him, observed in the work, or reported by
someone else. Pages were looked at in Chrome. Some 2004–2011 captures of his sites and of
Erskine Design and New Adventures were seen on the Wayback Machine before the research
rules excluded it; those findings are kept, and no capture was opened after that. No
video was used, so the talks stream rests on slide text and event descriptions. The notes
cite raw page text under `/tmp/designers/simon-collison/`, which was a local working
directory and is not included; each note lists its source URLs, so any page can be
fetched again.
