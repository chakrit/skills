# Dan Cederholm research

Research behind `style.md`: what Dan Cederholm (SimpleBits, co-founder of Dribbble) has
published openly about how he designs, what his own sites, goods and products show, and
what others report about his work, analysed for design thinking, type and taste rather
than code. Gathered November 2026 from freely available sources only: his site, shop and
newsletter, Dribbble, free articles and interviews, podcast transcripts and other
people's reviews. No paid books.

| Path           | Contents                                                       |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `style.md`     | The style's instructions                                       |
| `previews/`    | Self-contained pages showing the style                         |
| `synthesis.md` | The design model drawn from all four streams, citing each note |
| `notes/`       | One analysis per research stream, each with its source list    |

The streams:

| Note          | Covers                                                                   |
|---------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `own.md`      | simplebits.com today and in Wayback captures 2005–2016, fonts, notebook  |
| `work.md`     | Products and goods: Dribbble, font specimens, badges, prints, books      |
| `talks.md`    | Talks via recaps, A List Apart articles, interviews, podcast transcripts |
| `accounts.md` | Peers and reviewers on his templates, books, Cork'd, Dribbble and site   |

Every claim in the notes is marked as stated by him, observed in the work, or reported by
someone else. The `own` and `work` streams looked at pages in a browser on 2026-11-23;
the Wayback Machine was ruled out partway through, so archive coverage stops at the
captures already seen, and most client work from 2003 to 2010 was not viewed. The `talks`
and `accounts` streams used no browser and are second-hand on the visual side. Many
quotes passed through a summarising fetch. The notes cite raw page text under
`/tmp/designers/dan-cederholm/`, which was a local working directory and is not included;
each note lists its source URLs, so any page can be fetched again.
