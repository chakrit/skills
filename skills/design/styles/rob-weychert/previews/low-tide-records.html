Mara Quell, Slack Water

Slack water is the short stretch between tides when the sea stops moving. Mara Quell recorded her second album in it, on the shingle at Rock-a-Nore, between four and six in the morning over eleven days in March. You can hear the boats being winched up behind the piano on side one.

There are seven pieces. The longest, “Net Shops”, runs nineteen minutes and uses a single chord on a borrowed harmonium. The shortest is under two. Mara played everything except the clarinet, which is Tom Beale, who drove down from Leeds with it on the back seat.

We pressed 500 copies at Curved Pressings in Ramsgate. Once they are gone we will press more, but the cassettes are a single run and will not come back.
